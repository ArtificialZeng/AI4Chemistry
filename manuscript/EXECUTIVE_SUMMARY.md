# Executive Summary: `Lehmann-TDDFT v2.0`

**Causal Neural Self-Energy Lifting Overcomes the Adiabatic Approximation in Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory**

---

## Core Breakthrough

For four decades, production linear-response time-dependent density functional theory (TD-DFT) has relied on the adiabatic approximation ($f_{xc}(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}',\omega)\equiv f_{xc}(0)$), creating a shared mathematical barrier across three central frontiers of excited-state chemistry:
1. **Absence of genuine double excitations** (`0/28` detectable on QUESTDB 2025 for adiabatic TD-DFT and double-hybrid CIS(D) TD-DFT),
2. **Inability to describe Hund's-rule-violating inverted singlet–triplet (INVEST) emitters** ($\Delta E_{ST} = E_{S_1} - E_{T_1} < 0$), and
3. **Topological collapse of photochemical $S_1/S_0$ conical intersections** from codimension-2 $(3N-8)$-dimensional seams to $(3N-7)$-dimensional crossing seams with imaginary RPA instabilities and trivial Berry phase ($\gamma = 0$).

**Spin-complete `Lehmann-TDDFT v2.0`** resolves all three frontiers inside a single $O(N^3)$ operator framework by constructing a **gauge-equivariant, size-intensive Hermitian Feshbach–Löwdin lifted Hamiltonian** $\mathcal{H}_\theta^{(\sigma)}$ over density-fitted hybrid TD-DFT (`DF-PBE0/def2-SVP` RI-TDA) single-exciton states and **spin-complete bi-exciton manifolds**---explicitly incorporating both symmetric $^1(TT)_+$ and group-theory-required antisymmetric $^1(TT)_-$ singlet channels alongside physically anchored diagonal poles $D_\mu^{\text{phys}}$ (`use_antisym_singlet_channel=True, antisym_version=2, use_phys_D_anchor=True`). Exact Schur complementation yields a strictly Kramers–Kronig causal dynamical self-energy $\mathbf{\Sigma}_{xc}^\theta(\omega) = \mathbf{V}_\theta(\omega\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{D}_\theta)^{-1}\mathbf{V}_\theta^\top$ with zero global pooling and zero atomic-coordinate shortcuts.
